Bugs and Errors

This is a personal project by a hobbyist so expect it to be wonky - please note this is my first time building a full site and I haven’t fully optimised it. Some loading delays should be expected (especially in the indexes/on profile pages).

Please let me know if:

  • You feel the lag is to an unreasonable/disruptive amount.
  • You encounter any bugs (that aren’t solved by refreshing/tracing your steps or are otherwise easily repeatable).
  • You encounter any fetch error (e.g. ‘Character Not Found: The requested character does not exist.’ when trying to view a character’s profile).
