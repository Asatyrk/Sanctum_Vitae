Creator's Foreword

Hey there! I'm Arty - the creator of this world. Since all of my writing is usually presented in-character and in-canon, I've included this section as a little extra guide and exposition.


The basic idea of my universe - Sanctum Vitae - is that every other universe that wants to be cannon, technically is - and that everything is more or less hypothetical. The written version of how the universe was created is true for the characters inside, their specific memories and how it's addressed generally; but is equally as true for any other version of creation that other universes have. A character I have that lives inside of an alternate space is technically a clone of the original and shares a basic soul, even if they look slightly different, act slightly different or have entirely different rules. All AU versions of my characters are as real as the main canon - just either mean less to me or aren't really the one I focus on. This includes fandoms, DnD campaigns or friend's universes.


I often use my characters to explore deep psychological themes and that’s made the universe they exist in follow that trend very closely. Things aren't exactly consistent or specific and that's supposed to be intentional. Making rules allows me to break them, which makes for interesting storytelling if you ask me.


But yeah, long story short, some details can be a bit complex, mind fucky, or hard to fully understand - but I hope that plays more into the mystic than take away from it. I also hope you enjoy it! This has been a universe over ~10 years in the making and it’s very dear to me - Thank you for reading!
