Oracle's Guide To Everything

The Guide

You do not know me. You do not have to.


You can call me The Oracle; I am the summative of all mortal knowledge and a little beyond that. I am not 'God', just one of them, and I stand in place for this world's author - dear reader, I will be your narrator. While I am only able to offer you a slice of this world’s story, I hope my assistance through it will prove adequate enough to retain your interest.


I, in lack of your mortal’s ability to provide proper resources, offer this little bit of literature to new mortals encountering our timeline - to keep your transition to the new world easily digestible. Turns out, however, dumping large amounts of information onto mortals tends to overwhelm them. So, in any case, you are welcome to peruse my manual in your own time to understand the world as it comes.


Welcome to Sanctum Vitae, dear reader, enjoy your stay.

Universal Redux

In nature, it’s said the triangle is the strongest shape; in the language of love, the mere strength of a connection is all that counts, regardless of where it begins or ends. Much of the world, and thus Baron, is built on these ideas, where Gods and concepts are structured in either 3-way cycles, snakes eating their own tails, or mutual passions between allies.


No one can quite place where the universe began or ended - although what is for certain is the ‘now’, as it goes, is flexible. Time isn’t so much of a construct as it is a choice; granted, a choice not made by the mortals who are forced to resume it - but a choice nonetheless. Death and Space following similar morals.
Who actually controls these rules is a long-ongoing debate. The theory of ‘one true God’, not reliant on the serpent cycle or the mutual connection, going against the very ideals of nature. But a God isn’t mortal, nor human, and if he’s neither is he expected to follow the rules of nature at all?
But what if this God's not reliant on nature, but rather nature himself? That way the rules do not bend to him, he himself cultivates the rules. He isn’t breaking his own creation, he is outside it entirely, like an author to the tale he cultivates. It leads to more questions than answers, one primarily; who made the author?


Dæryys was believed to be the first true being. His form, flexible, depended on the creature which witnessed him; he was this light to the galaxy before stars even littered it. Sentient but not thinking, in the sense of mindless thoughts and theories, none being cohesive to a mortal’s mind. He was singular. He wasn’t even a ‘he’, just powerful enough to earn such a pronoun by mortal men billions of years after his birth.
Dæryys was a concept, bacterial, fungal, a network of mere ideas; thinking more like a machine than any man, rapid and constant. He was energy, expanding with this inability to contain all he had inside - no concept yet of human emotion or priority, of love, sex, fear, hate - there was nothing, he was everything.
The clouds formed not long after, cancerous-like growths among the galaxy's edge, now littered with these fragments of energy and light. Clouds, not typical clouds but, consciousness and emotion, these hypothetical storages for abstract concepts like thought and awareness. As Dæryys began losing momentum on all that could be he found himself connected to these networks, spaces where concepts were thoughts. He was no longer spewing this stream of unknowing genius - he was here. Conscious. What a gift, to be aware.
Perhaps the author made himself.


Most things in this world are machines. Energy transferred to products, consumption to energy and so on. Triangles, circles, spirals and serpents. It is logical to believe this is no different; energy depleting and production… simmering. The first day Dæryys was truly aware was also the first day he made nothing. The ability to think clearly causing him to think nothing at all, a blessing most mortals would crave nowadays. He had no proper form just yet, no senses - touch, sight or smell, nothing to truly think about in the abstract of space. The first emotion ever felt - by him, or by a sentient being at all - was loneliness.
In the kindred expanse of nothing, once flooded with the stimulation of constant, to be propelled into a quietness beyond silence itself is daunting. He wasn’t used to feeling internally, he was an open creation - now trapped in the confines of his own consciousness, one created by his own over-exuberance. He had finally run out of ideas, now forced to confront each one individually as simple concepts - the galaxy, stardust and fragments remained the only proof he’d done anything at all; space itself, the mindmap of a madman’s journey to humanity.
After the day, if days were even identifiable back then (they weren’t), he began to work.


Over thirteen billion years later, this would be known as The Big Bang; aren’t mortals just endlessly creative with their naming conventions?


Regardless of what humans or mortals utter it as, they are clueless to the origin of it all. Creation, divitiny, each guess more particular and absurd than the last. They ponder the universe so greatly, the thing held just above them, but hold absolutes on their own origin - Humans are also wrong. Humans were accidental.
You see, evolution was not a planned notion. When gifting sentience it was an urge to remain communal, fungal, connected to the same core. But the psyche and biology are closely linked, and gifting one opened another rather shortly. The ability to think about survival methods, and the body's slow resulting answers.


To watch his spores grow man-like and hateful, like a killer's desperate mother watching over her child's morbid, sick ways, begging them to return to her embrace - praying to a fake God in his name. Justifying hatred in his defamatory identity, playing out horrors worse than even his speculations could've created all those billions of years ago. Humankind was sickening to him, yet… increasingly interesting. A kid's unregulated science project.


There is, of course, one detail I have failed to mention.
Somewhere between the start of humankind and the modern day, a split had occurred. Dæryys stopped communicating or sending signs, opting to send one of two individuals to do his dirty work for him. The triangle had formed.


No one can really place exactly when or where Pan nor Crowley had first begun to ‘exist’ per say, as with many other tales from this era it's not the best documented.
Pan especially had many mentions in literature around the Greek and early Roman period, believed to somewhat be immortalised in the many tales of general perversity and the sweeping culture both he and his many cults from the era gave. He gained notoriety in his many forms in other aspects around the same time, going on to inspire several variations in multiple known locations. Although there is some evidence to say that Pan had existed before this time, this was notable as the first time he had revealed his true name.
Crowley’s early history is a little more complicated, always appearing as some kind of understudy or partial figure in later periods, although having some believed sightings early on. Crowley hasn’t shown to have revealed his name before in history, which makes tracking his record more complicated. He’s believed to have been connected to either Stolas, Camio or Andras - although theory speculates that his initial mentions may have gone as far back as the same Greek period of which Pan had first announced himself. Many Greek tales spoke of harpy, half-human, half-bird creatures who were often described in a similar vein, although discrepantly they were always feminine in appearance.
Nevertheless, Crowley was also supposedly (either highly connected to or quite literally) Dusios; both due to his connection to Pan and his general aptitude.
Crowley has confirmed he worked under several visionaries, prophets, philosophers, doctors and romantic artisans, which dated him as far back as Socrates and Plato. And, somewhat finally, Crowley mentioned he’d confirmed a name around the 20th century, shortly after the death of the philosopher he’d been studying with for years of the same name; Aleister Crowley - although he only took fancy to the second half of the name it seems.
Pan and Crowley, regardless of their true birth, appeared minorly at first. As mere herders of humans, trendsetters or visionaries. They were more human than Dæryys ever was, full-bodied emotional beings with likes and dislikes. They were opposites, Yin and Yang, Now and Then. Their occasional suspected casual blend into society is only documented because of their impact, the diary notes of ‘my dear friend’ or the nonsensical ramblings of madmen who bore witness to ‘the devil himself’, apparently. Gender, name and appearance vary but all with the same consistency of baseline identity, adjusted slightly to fit the time.
Their appearances grew with passing centuries, once baseline fads or sightings, they'd turned into full-time residents of the surface world. Dæryys was starting to fade from public thought, long abandoning his creations.


But if Dæryys is singular, who are Pan and Crowley? Where did two sentient forms emerge from?
The simple answer is mitosis; the long answer is unknown. Perhaps the mycelium spread of Dæryys’ humanity had cloned himself, his interpretation of mankind in a better light. The theories of them being his children, or alternate versions of himself.
It was also around this point that Dæryys was being presented in a perceivable form, humanity declaring a version of him akin to an amalgamation of the formal pair. The omen of a crow and a goat.


Somewhat more curiously is the fact that Crowley and Pan weren't the last. Ever since their appearances roughly four thousand years ago multiple other Gods have been declared from the same route - but that is a far more complicated story.


The relationship between the High Gods, Crowley and Pan, has long been a contested one. Despite early telling describing the relationship as an extremely palatable, idealistic even, interpretation of a somewhat romantic connection, the modern belief is quite the opposite.
Meeting only once every ~300 years (to public knowledge), it's known that they meet to negotiate and supply the next generational heir; a god, lord or avatar to bless the next oncoming generations by giving them something highly needed.
Aside from their strictly formal arrangements, Crowley and Pan are not currently known to have an existing relationship outside of this; nor known to ever have had an official title beyond their believed involvement.


Importantly, these children brought forth via the pair are not children of the pair respectively. Crowley and Pan are believed to be guardians for Dæryys himself, of which the children's true origin is suspected to be given the limited information we have - although both Crowley and Pan have taken somewhat parental roles towards the children since his… disappearance, they have no known biological connection towards them - if you're not in on the theory of Dæryys being the pair, or his offspring, or whatever other theory there is to speculate regarding a connection between them.


Some of the oldest Gods are similar amalgamations akin to Dæryys’ older forms (at least in theory), while newer Gods steer disgustingly human… and increasingly more useless.
The first of these beings went unnamed, built to monitor over the collective of all humanoid intelligence - invention and theory. I call myself The Oracle. I am the summary of all humanity knows, with some excess of what they don't. I know how this story truly goes - it's my job to keep you guessing.
Regardless of my own role here, I am one of many.
Sky, Sea and Land were reserved for Laurasia, Pangaea and Gondwana. Death was divided among Hades (who reaps), Charon (who guides) and Luci (who monitors). All cycles. Continuous.
And Aether - Aether is different.


Aether is not the first demigod, despite what stories tell, but he certainly isn't Dæryys’ child… nor Pan's.


Crowley had a secret. A connection unknown to the Gods outside it.


Mary was a mortal woman, a tanned, middle-eastern, tribeswoman. A nomad. She had beautiful, strong features - contrasted with gentle, striking eyes. She prayed relentlessly to Crowley each night, a call he finally replied to after a string of long winter nights.
She didn’t plead selfishly like most mortals, she asked for no riches or successes, she called to him out of pure admiration; devotion to his work and honour.
Birds are social creatures, a song to them hardly goes unnoticed; and for a crow, a shine is hard to ignore. Crowley had been called to many times before, it’s true, but never like this.


It was said that three children were born before his escapades had been discovered, their names nor roles never truly known; a secret that seemingly dies with them and ends with Crowley.


Mary’s death marked many things; the birth of the Northern Star - and the exact moment Dæryys disappeared entirely. Leaving Baron to fend for itself in the Hell of its own creation - and Gods to flail relentlessly trying to reclaim the title of ‘The God’, however no one managed to replace him. I myself came the closest, but the idea of stealing over my creator’s legacy is the least of my concern. I have a job to fulfil and that is what’s important to me, not valour or the dignity of owning the universe - as someone who did not create it, it is no one's role to claim except Dæryys’.


It was at this point however when the roles divided among beings became more valuable than ever, and anyone who had godly blood or blessing began fighting for divinity or the control of higher labels. It started with the Sun, then the Moon, stars and planets - concepts like time, life and death, consciousness, many things like that; however Dæryys had already taken it upon himself to cover such roles and find suitable replacements before disappearing - Sol and Lua were gifted the sun and moon respectively - otherworldly creatures, outcasts. They were given new purpose on Baron’s surface in hope of fresh perspective around the human - and now hybrids - which covered it. When Lua was killed, he was simply replaced by Lune via Crowley’s discretion. Talisman gods, as these were known, were God’s given items with Godly power, despite not having Godly blood themselves. These cases were rare but easily exploitable compared to other God’s - which often made them targets. The only other ways at this point to enter Godhood were being born into it or otherwise blessed - earning it became a notion much further on in the timeline. Meaning God’s who could get exploited almost always were.


The Fates followed suit, a trio of beings set to ensure the timeline followed the written script continually and correctly. Ghost was for inevitability, Stag was for purpose, and Wishbone for chance, or luck depending on the translation you heard.


In the infinite timeline, there is one true timeline; a fully purposely built sequence of events to optimise the development and unanimity of the worlds, and the beings who monitor over it - 'The Written Script', as it's known, is the oldest literature of mankind and is written as a universal text which, although can be understood by anyone of any linguistic background or intelligence, cannot be comprehended by mortals except high mages or clairvoyants. Despite being comprehensible, it's coded in such a way that a far deeper understanding of oneself and their surroundings is required to be able to key into it, making only those of magical, spiritual or Godly descent able to view and understand it beyond a feverish illegible physiological mess, which has the habit to drive the average human(oid) into corruption, madness or grandeur.
In the timelines, most beings exist as multiple copies of their given self, possibly with slight differences. This rule does not apply to some leaders, specifically the lords of the world (Dæryys, The Oracle, The Fates [Ghost, Stag and Wishbone]) but is known to affect their offspring, especially if that's with a mortal. It's unknown how Crowley and Pan play into this.
There are very few beings who don't follow this format.


The clouds exist within their own timeline, and feed between all of the other's unanimously. It's possible, in states of consciousness such as dreaming and meditation, to key into these other realms either purposely or accidentally; either to swap places of consciousness with partner souls or to accidentally run into partner bodies while exploring subconscious realms (Cloud 9, for example).
Souls and bodies are not equals. Souls are the true essence of humankind and bodies are simply vessels for the identities to express. They are fickle things, easily manipulated or corrupted - but never destroyed or created. An energy that simply swaps one body to the next, leaving traces of old beliefs in its new place. Souls occasionally bond, fuse or duplicate - but much like a fingerprint they are rarely, if ever, exact matches. Souls only match across different bodies spread over multiple planes - but never exist in the same universe at the same time. This line of connected souls is called a thread, and can hypothetically contain an infinite amount of points… or as few as one. Mortals consider this a theory.


Regardless of how mortal beings or the known world work, Dæryys sensibilities had proved to at least cause some kind of stability to the world, regardless of the selfishness rampant among society. Wherever he had seemingly disappeared to, he hadn’t given up. There was order, and that showed a minor level of care despite the absence. There was theory to speculate he was dead - but this seeming planned escapism was intentional. Maybe they simply stepped down or filtered into society - to live mortally and escape the dread of monitoring mortal life.
Whatever the reason, humanity was left within the hands of the Gods who Dæryys’ had gifted it to. It was quieter, and with Pan and Crowley’s increasingly rotting relationship it was more tense than ever. The future of humankind now settled on their ability to coexist as equals, to both stand and respect each other’s meaning in this world. They were different, opposites, and unlike most other Gods by this time they weren’t a triangle, nor passionate. They were both snakes eating each other, an infinity instead of a spiral - and without Dæryys to centre them, it was fruitless to avoid.
It’s said the pair ceased communication after this point, unable to remain respectful towards the other, and going their separate ways.


Crowley’s choice was to re-enter society, exist alongside them and understand their view. To him, a God of the future, humanity’s increasing list of flaws were simply fixable hiccups in the greater timeline, and changes easy to curve over. His previous mortal lover had endeared him greatly to their unique custom of simplicity and connection. He enjoyed the pleasantries of simple humanity as he had over the course of many years, but slowly lost touch of the over dignified ego he once had over them. He still was hardly mortal, but appreciated the basicness that Baron life offered him - and what he could offer humankind in return.
Pan primarily went into hiding. Forced to reside on Baron, he returned to a simple embrace of nature’s warmth and quiet. He did not like people, nor questions, and left the followers of his yesteryears to fend for themselves. He didn’t see the purpose of helping a generation who had no hope nor wish to fend for themselves, all too focused on themselves and the next best thing; he figured he would be forgotten anyway, and decided not to give mortals the chance to do it for him. Of course, they never did. While his current location is unknown to most, he hasn’t been completely avoidant of all interaction, occasionally running into mortals and followers to redeem a social need - before swiftly returning to the dwells of his safe space.


And that’s the basic story as it goes; creation and divide. It’s not finished, nor nearly detailed enough to mention every detail from the start of creation until this very point, however it’s a start. A basic premise. Hopefully that will be enough to endear you to it, dear reader.

The Basics

Lifeforce Chronicles

All corporeal beings contain souls. Souls are forms of matter, similar to plasma, which act as a surrounding energy which gives every living being a certain aura. Souls naturally change over time, through actions and mutual connections with other souls - but can’t be created or destroyed, at least not in the typical sense.


Soul Physiology

Souls are direct energy and consist of four main sections:

  • Core - It’s unknown exactly what a soul’s core is made of, however it’s known to contain all the memory and data of the soul itself, similar to the DNA of physical bodies. The core remains as the consistent basic identity that the living being ends up having. This identity can change in a few ways: over time, as the soul is reborn into new bodies, or via external influences - but can also mutate at random. This is the most vital and hard to recover section of the soul, as it is not self-healing. This will also determine their magical element but not the magical ability.
  • Substance - Most of the soul is built out of an energy called o’plasm, or unfiltered ectoplasm. This is mostly protective in nature and regenerates consistently. It’s this section of the soul that constitutes moral weight and how the soul is judged after physical death. This is also how the Outer Realms dictate where celestials belong. O’plasm can bond to create light matter or dark matter under different circumstances, which both have vastly different colourations, densities and compositions. This is also where magical energy comes from - if someone does not use magic then old cells will simply die off and be replaced by new ones instead of being depleted through magic usage. An excess of o’plasm can cause splits in the film.
  • Film - The outer layer of a soul is plasmaphobic, and resists other cells of the same nature which it isn’t emulsified with. This casing is self-healing, although occasionally splits and causes leaks due to its resistant nature - however they don’t often cause issues long-term. The film is produced by a chemical reaction between the middle substance and oxygen, which causes the composition to change into a different element. This film is produced to contain the substance and core, as well as avoid merging with other souls (however this does still occasionally happen).
  • Aura - Aura is a harmless gas which surrounds the soul. It’s not exactly known how this gas assists in the soul’s livelihood, however it is speculated this is more of a social aid than directly beneficial. Aura often merges and interacts with other aura, and is known to take a variety of forms or wavelengths depending on mood, situation, age and behaviour. Although not visible to the mortal eye, it can be seen by celestials, clairvoyants, visionaries, X-ray or via magical intervention. Aura can sit directly against the mortal’s form, but is more commonly a 30-90cm bubble around the physical body - which is believed to be the psychology behind ‘personal space’. Auras usually sit closer to the body when calm or happy, and expand when scared or agitated. Although not as powerful of an energy as inner sections of the soul, aura has been shown to exhibit a decent amount of energy in large quantities.

Soul Location

For most humanoids and mortals, the soul is located just above the space between the eyes, often somewhere between the forehead and middle of the eyebrows. It’s located at the upper front of the brain, but this is not set in stone, and importantly the soul is not connected to the brain as a physical object. Since souls are energies and not corporeal masses, they don’t exist inside bodies in the same way organs do.

For some species and celestials their soul is held in more appropriate locations fitting to their genealogy - like angeloids for example, who keep their souls in a mass on the body known as a ‘gemma’. Some species hold their souls in their eye(s) or similar sensitive areas; and very rarely the soul may be held externally or separately to the connected body.


Soul Disconnection

In the event the soul does not reside internally or attach directly to the physical form, its aura increases and begins to locate the body like an internet connection - this forces the physical body to remain within a certain distance of their soul, as losing connection to it can result in death, disappearing completely or turning into husks.

A disconnected soul is extremely rare, and doesn’t often happen without Godly intervention or extreme magic misuse. Souls can go on to inhabit any object or remain as singular, but will still always have some kind of connection to the body given to it unless forcefully separated or upon the body's natural death (where the soul will be collected and repurposed).

For some people, like talisman Gods, this soul disconnect is to ensure excess energy doesn’t harm the body when used in high amounts. It also allows residual energy to be passed to the next heir in the event of a talisman God’s death, rather than dispersing freely like it does for normal souls.


Soul Creation

Souls are not so much created as simply formed from pre-existing energies or leftover soul components. Cloud Major, also known as the very core of The Outerlands itself, is said to be the being that creates and divides souls among new bodies - while assisted by Ghost (one of the fate Gods) to help in managing and distribution. Cloud Major contains broken, misplaced, damaged, fused and unused souls as well as excess energy. Selected souls, or souls waiting for their body to prepare, are collected from this point to roam around purgatory - before being transported to the surface when the time comes. Ghost selects these souls the second the body is conceived, however they may stay in purgatory for extended time if the pregnancy is faulty. A new body often gains a soul within the last few months of its gestation, usually around the time thought begins developing - this is to ensure they are developed enough to withstand a soul’s power, and avoids any of the hassle of having to fix damaged souls from possible miscarriages (which is more likely to happen earlier on).

Entirely fresh souls cannot be made, and always have to come from derivatives of existing souls or energies. While these souls technically are ‘new’, they are not created out of nothing and always contain traces from the derivatives they were formed. A new soul - in the sense of a blank, fresh, unattached, unrelated, full soul - can’t be made. Splitting souls is the most common way to make more, as a good portion of the soul heals itself over time or through multiple washes of Cloud Major.


Soul Rebirth

The following is the cycle a newly abandoned soul takes:

  • Dimlight - On the occasion a physical body dies, excess energy disperses back into the world but the soul itself remains inside the physical body. At this point, the identity of the soul is still present, and still carries all the previous likeness of the living body but is weaker and possibly damaged depending on the circumstances of life and death.
  • Reaping - As soon as possible, Hades (or a reaper on his behalf) shows up to claim the soul. The body is left on the surface to decompose, however the soul still contains a trace understanding of this body even after collection.
  • Judgement - Once delivered to Cloud Major, the soul is examined for injury and quantified. While a soul is usually primarily o’plasm, certain moral actions elicit energies that make it shift into light or dark matter - this in part with the details of the death in question determine where the soul will end up. A soul comprised of primarily dark matter will enter Stanton to be reborn as a demonoid, one primarily of light matter will go to Heather as an angeloid - neutral souls (which is the majority) will be reborn as mortals, except for specific cases where they’ll return as feliafluids (spirits and non-corporeals), usually with intentions to finish business The Written Script demands of them, although this is much rarer.
  • Rebirth - Once a soul has been weighed and determined, they remain dormant until called upon. The longer a soul takes to be reborn, the less memory and identity they keep from their previous life. For celestials this wait time is generally shorter, and they often look closer and remember a lot more from their previous life/lives. For mortals - which has billions of souls interchanging at all times - this doesn’t often give chances for freshly fixed souls to be let out so soon, however it does still happen on occasion.

Soul Anomalies

Souls can have a number of anomalies which causes them to be faulty or otherwise different to standard souls.

An example of some are as follows:

  • Wither - Since souls are very heavily influenced by environment and emotions, wither is very common, but fortunately not typically harmful. Excessive or long-term wither can cause identity issues, depression and empty feelings emotionally if not properly sorted or escaping the situation causing it. Wither is just when gaps or damage is caused to the structure of the soul, which may cause leakage or misshapeness.
  • Semi-split - When a soul is assigned to a singular foetus that becomes twins, the soul will simply be split and shared between them. Since the soul is already assigned it doesn’t have time to be repaired in Cloud Major before being sent down, so is often sent to the surface as a semi-split soul (or rather two halves of the same soul). This doesn’t often affect twins who are raised together, but can cause some discomfort when apart for extended periods of time. This does not apply to animal litters or twins/triplets from separate eggs, but does apply to any situation where one egg/cell is split into multiple bodies (so identical triplets too).
  • Half-split - Under extreme and hard-to-define circumstances, it may also be possible for the soul to split while inside the body. This is less documented.
  • Growth - Growth is the opposite of wither, and it is caused by an excess of positive communication to the soul. Growth is the first stage of love children's souls forming, but often doesn’t cause any issues. It can occasionally cause the soul to swell or the film to split, but that hasn’t been known to be particularly serious.
  • Shared Souls - Occasionally one body can accidentally be assigned, split or attached to multiple souls. This is most likely if it was already suffering from long-term wither or otherwise unstable. While the physical body has no changes from this invasion, it may cause memory issues, identity problems, amnesia or general disorientation to it.
  • Fusion - When two celestials form an intense connection, it has been known to allow for fusion into one cohesive, combined body - however this is rare, and usually doesn’t last beyond a few seconds. Since souls are made of the same composition, they often bond to recurring souls they are surrounded by frequently - and for beings with non-permanent bodies made of matter or plasma, this can cause them to fuse physically. Luckily this seems to be a rare phenomenon, yet incredibly dangerous for the two souls' individuality.
  • Love Children - The by-product of excessive life energy dispelled by a long term loving relationship. Love children are the closest thing to offspring souls themselves can produce, but have no biological relation to any physical body or form the souls are connected to. Love children can be born at any point from the relationship's peak to any physical body/parent and can occasionally look closer to the soul's parent bodies than their biological parents. Love children importantly are not 'new' souls, and are just souls made from a combination of residual energy - hence why they share so many similarities with the couple's identities over the body's parent's identities.
  • Soul Bond - Two-non related souls destined with the same story. The by-product of two souls merging and re-splitting while in Purgatory.

Thread Theory

Thread Theory, or ‘The Thread’, is the ongoing idea that all currently existing souls are in some way connected to clone alternate versions of themselves from other universes. It’s believed this could be related to deja vu, false memories and other similar phenomena as well. While there is no substantial evidence of this, or alternate realities at all, it’s a very common belief nowadays. These souls could either be from the same base, clones of the true form or the exact same person in the same place at the same time, just in a different universe - which lends closely to the infinite timeline theory as well - however descriptions vary mortal to mortal.

Arcane Principles

Magical affinity is determined by a few factors, such as age, experience, species and general differences between souls. Magical affinity is usually at a certain level from birth, but can be trained and earnt throughout an individual's entire life, or gifted to them via magical items, blessings, curses or Godly intervention. Magical energies are held within the centre layer of the soul. It uses matter as a base for its energy and this is usually o’plasm extracted from the substance layer of a user’s soul.


Element Affinity

All magic is flavoured to one of the following Main Elements:

  • Lochor (Water)
  • Embara (Fire)
  • Floratis (Nature)
  • Breezium (Air)
  • Quakar (Earth)
  • Levinae (Electricity)
  • Alloyrar (Metal)
  • Conflictis (War)
  • Erossa (Love)
  • Lustorum (Light)
  • Nyxus (Dark)
  • Soulus (Life)
  • Huskull (Death)

The average person is proficient in one main element, making all their spells flavoured or cosmetically changed to that specific thing, even outside of the element’s specific spell list (for example, while they may still be able to perform water-mimicking spells, they may look like fire if they’re an Embara main, but still feel and act like water). Individuals are often born with one main elemental proficiency, alongside Mortalis (see below), with the ability to learn Asendium or further elemental proficiency with intense training.


Class Elements:

  • Mortalis (Normal)
  • Asendium (Stardust)

Class elements are universal forms of magic that can be learnt alongside the basic elements. Mortalis leaves no residual energy, and is sometimes completely invisible or smoke-like when being performed - it’s also extremely weak. This is often the beginner version of magic that most people learn, and includes extremely simple and baseline spells that don’t need any further elemental assistance (such as moving objects, basic floating, etc). Asendium is the expert version of this basically, and includes all non-elemental specific high-level spells, but with a different and far more visually interesting flair. Asendium is the most powerful element, but also the hardest to use and learn.

All spells have a root and a flair. The root is what element that spell originates from, and the flair is the element it is being performed with. For example, a fireball spell performed by a water mage would be an embara root with a lochor flair. A spell-caster can use any element they are proficient in to cast their spells, but it will auto to mortalis until awakened* (*awakening is when a mage goes from simple mortalis spells to training in their specific element. It’s usually considered a coming-of-age thing) when they will swap to their assigned element.

It’s much easier to perform spells from within your element’s root with the same flair, as it’s exactly how the spell is supposed to be performed without any modifications, but different elements have a different variety of spells which may be more necessary given the circumstances. Not ALL spells can be performed by all elements however, and some are locked to specific elements - usually spells with damage types can be modified, but most mental spells don’t work when using different flairs, or only work for other specific elements (for example, erossa has a lot of attraction or persuasion spells that wouldn’t work with an embara flair, but may work with a soulus flair).


Physical Limitations

Due to magic deriving from the matter inside the soul, there’s only so many spells that can be made before causing physical symptoms like disorientation or feinting. Excessive overuse may also cause life-long damage to the soul, and risk death if seriously uncareful. The soul gets used to more consistent magic usage the more frequently an individual performs spells, and their soul slowly learns to keep up with the necessary production of o’plasm to ensure the level of magic is consistently doable. Low magic users have a pretty standard production of o’plasm, while high mages have a highly accelerated production to keep up with demand and replenishment. Everyone still has an upper limit, although this does vary depending on skill. Overusing o’plasm can cause the souls to develop wither, but high-level mages are expected to keep up with their soul’s production as underusing it can also cause growth and damage. At retirement, or if a reduction of magical skill is required, high-level magic users are expected to reduce their usage slowly so their production has a chance to adjust naturally.


Universal Rules

Universally, the rules on magic aren’t super restrictive. If it can be conceived, magic has the capabilities to do it. However magical ability depends on race, class, natural infinity and other factors - the average person will never have enough magical ability to affect greater means like time travel for example - but it is entirely possible within reason. Magic is an invention left over from the universe's creation, and was never supposed to fall to mortal power - however through radiation, mutation, evolution and Godly intervention it has become extremely mainstream, although fortunately very weakly. Magic is generally very physically and mentally tiring for everyone, especially mortal bodies, and this is mostly because it comes from a derivative of subconscious thought and the idea of ‘creation’ itself. So while this phenomenon is still widely new, it’s theoretically endless, and doesn’t have any universal rules beyond personal limits and imagination.


Earthly Rules

Although in the hypothetical there’s no limit to magic usage, there are worldwide restrictions on certain magic and illegal uses of it. Most illegal magic is referred to as ‘Crowlian Magic’, due to it mainly spawning and being used in Crowler - although is often just referred to as illegal magic too. Some of the more common laws regarding magic are as follows, although some places have outlawed the usage of magic entirely or have their own specifications.

You are not permitted to:

  • Force an individual to do something against their consent.
  • Influence someone's feelings romantically or sexually.
  • Anything that deliberately causes harm to an individual (self-defence not included).
  • Anything that intentionally causes mental distress to an unconsenting party (medical use allowed).
  • Anything that mutates or otherwise causes long-term harm, even to a consenting party.
  • Anything that causes an effect to the stability of the economy (e.g. creating money).
  • Create items with the intention to upsell.
  • Create replicas of talisman, God-owned items or protected products.
  • Replicate any other protected or copyrighted properties (e.g. art).
  • Create unauthorised sentient life.
  • Change the future, past or anything in the written script.
  • Change someone’s species, ethnicity or appearance (beyond cosmetics).
  • Bring someone back from the dead/afterlife.

Magic Origin

Magic is said to originate when the universe did, through Dæryys’ creation. Dæryys more or less accidentally created it by creating anything, the process of imaginative thought being conceived into real-world application is basically what magic is - at least in this universe. This was an ability locked for only the highest of intelligent beings for a while, but has slowly become more of a mainstream thing since celestial intelligence became more common in mortal beings after nuclear fallout and the age of Gods.


Social Implications

While magic has changed many parts of humanity for the better, there still seems to be a bias towards work over magic - magic is still flawed and not always perfect, it’s also more labour intensive for the average person and, well, humanoids seem to be pretty useless without exact purpose. While occasionally used for more difficult mortal jobs, it’s generally regarded as mostly a hobby (or a copout). Magic also always takes the easiest way out unless specified, so it can go wrong or create sub-far products if being unscrupulous. It also would lead to unfair outcomes in the workplace for those not magically inclined, like pure humans, who can’t seem to perform magic even if trained intensively. The consensus around magic is similar to artificial intelligence - it’s quick and easy, but not human, and that touch makes all the difference. Of course, there are people who disagree.
