Oracle's Guide To Everything

The Guide

You do not know me. You do not have to. You can call me The Oracle; I am the summative of all mortal knowledge and a little beyond that. I am not 'God', just one of them, and I stand in place for this world's author - dear reader, I will be your narrator. While I am only able to offer you a slice of this world’s story, I hope my assistance through it will prove adequate enough to retain your interest. Welcome to Sanctum Vitae, dear reader, enjoy your stay.
