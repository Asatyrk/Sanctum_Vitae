Glossary

Places & Nationalities

Old Earth - The original Earth, now Mars. Overrun by zombies and animals.

Baron - Earth 2.0, with a layout similar to our planet but with some minor additions/changes where most of my lore takes place.

Crowler - A collection of islands above Norway. Run by TPTOG + TCoC.

  • Crowlian - The nationality of Crowler.
  • Salvor - The main island of Crowler.
  • Sol & Lune - The secondary islands of Crowler.
  • Dæryys' Portal - The more rural but second largest island of Crowler.
  • Brandon's Lagoon - The far offshore, smallest island of Crowler.

Rotaur - A nation bordering the East Siberian Sea. Run by TPTOG.

  • Rotaurian - The nationality of Rotaur.
  • Upper Rotaur - The upper-class and rich region of Rotaur.
  • Lower Rotaur - The lower-class and poor region of Rotaur.

Veronix - An island close to the Somali basin. Does not have an official governing party (considered lawless).

  • Veroxin - The nationality of Veronix.

Aestrou - A collection of 5 man-made islands in the Atlantic Ocean. Run by TCoC.

  • Aestrouin - The general nationality of Aestrou, specific names are usually used instead (see below).
  • Dawn - One of the nations of Aestrou
    • Dawnese - The nationality of Dawn.
  • Dusk - One of the nations of Aestrou
    • Dusken - The nationality of Dusk
  • Knight - One of the nations of Aestrou
    • Knighten - The nationality of Knight
  • Tyme - One of the nations of Aestrou
    • Tymenian - The nationality of Tyme
  • Twilux - One of the nations of Aestrou
    • Twiluxan - The nationality of Twiluxan

The Outer Realms - A collection of realms outside the regular planet alignment, mostly the afterlife in its many forms.

  • Stanton - One of the afterlives, similar to 'Hell'.
    • Stantonese - The nationality of Stanton.
  • Purgatory - A temporary holding space for souls, ghosts, and those waiting for reincarnation.
  • Heather - One of the afterlives, similar to 'Heaven'.
    • Heather - The nationality of Heather
  • Cloud 9 - A realm of collective human consciousness.
  • Deadspace - A place implied to exist but not humanly understood, only known to be used by Gods (if real at all).

Tesga - Alien planet.

  • Tesgan - The nationality of Tesga.

Factions & Companies

PTO Government (TPTOG) - Long name: ‘The Protective Tyrannical Order’. One of the 2 groups that monitors Crowler. TPTOG also monitors over Rotaur. Has a poor reputation.

  • The Kristian-Brauwn Society (KBS) - A subsection of the PTO Government that specializes in technology and medicine, run by Diablo Kristian and Finch Brauwn.
  • The Species Protection Order (SPO) - A subsection of the PTO Government that specializes in protecting public services and ensuring all species live communally. Has a reputation for not treating people correctly or doing inhumane practices (like de-tusking orcs/goblins).

The Council of Crowns (TCoC) - One of the 2 groups that monitors Crowler. TCoC also monitors over Aestrou. Generally preferred by the public.


Religious & Spiritual

The Virtues - A group of religions.

  • Virtue of Worship - A section of the virtues that has specific beliefs but doesn't follow any particular God/Gods.
  • Virtue of Service - A section of the virtues that has specific beliefs but doesn't follow any particular God/Gods - has different beliefs than worship and is often viewed as lesser than the other virtues (and more cult-ish).
  • Virtue of Pan - A section of the virtues that worship Pan specifically.
  • Virtue of Crowley - A section of the virtues that worship Crowley specifically.

Godex - Gender-neutral version of God or Goddess.

God Terms - Multiple terms are used for Gods/God-like positions, as follows:

  • High God - A title used for a select few; the closest thing to 'THE' God. Monitors over the universe as a whole.
  • God - A higher being who monitors over a large-scale concept. Has the ability to control it directly. Also used as a blanket term for all other God-like titles (all below)
  • Lord - A higher being who monitors over a large-scale concept. Can't control it directly, but has some small influence towards it.
  • Avatar - A higher being who monitors over a smaller, more specific concept. Can't control it directly and instead has to protect it.
  • Patron - A God, Lord or Avatar that gains powers through a belief system or communal energy. Will die if the concept becomes obsolete.
  • Lich Patron - A God, Lord or Avatar that gains powers through a forcefully gained belief system (such as a cult) or by rerouting communally-gained energy into their own concept. This gained power is often parasitic and not by the choice of the ‘feeding’ God. Lich Patrons are extremely uncommon and are usually caused by greed from dying patrons unable to get the required energy from their own worship circle, but Lich Patrons can also be made through magical means.
  • Deity - A mortal who gained God-like abilities through their own work (e.g Wizards) or otherwise accidentally (curse, blessing, etc).
  • Talisman God - A mortal who gained God-like abilities after being gifted a talisman (magical artefact) from another God. Will die if the talisman is stolen or destroyed.
  • Demigod - A half God, usually from one mortal parent and one God parent. Usually given a smaller role.
  • Semigod - Not fully mortal but not quiet God-enough to be trusted with power. Also the title given to demigods who don't work.

Souls & Magic

Spirit Bond - Two-non related souls destined with the same story. The by-product of two souls merging and re-splitting while in Purgatory.

Love Child - A being created by the intense bond of a relationship. The by-product of excessive life energy dispelled by a long term loving relationship. Love children are the closest thing to offspring souls themselves can produce, but have no biological relation to any physical body or form the souls are connected to. Love children can be born at any point from the relationship's peak to any physical body/parent and can occasionally look closer to the soul's parent bodies than their biological parents. Love children importantly are not 'new' souls, and are just souls made from a combination of residual energy - hence why they share so many similarities with the couple's identities over the body's parent's identities.

Clothing

Vallion - A Crowlian piece of gear used as an under layer for warmth and extra padding. A skin-tight turtleneck which cups the crotch like a swimsuit, occasionally has cut-outs on the chest. Always black or dark-coloured. Must be made with specifically the wool of stantonshires.


Social

"Aye Us" - A term started by Pan and Crowley, an exclamatory replacement for 'Oh God' or 'Jesus Christ' or similar sayings. Often used by higher up Gods as an inside joke. Mortals/lesser Gods will often say 'Oh Gods' or 'Aye Dæryys' instead.
