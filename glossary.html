Glossary

Places & Nationalities

Old Earth - The original Earth, now Mars. Overrun by zombies and animals.

Baron - Earth 2.0, with a layout similar to our planet but with some minor additions/changes where most of my lore takes place.

Crowler - A collection of islands above Norway. Run by TPTOG + TCoC.

  • Crowlian - The nationality of Crowler.
  • Salvor - The main island of Crowler.
  • Sol & Lune - The secondary islands of Crowler.
  • Dæryys' Portal - The more rural but second largest island of Crowler.
  • Brandon's Lagoon - The far offshore, smallest island of Crowler.

Rotaur - A nation bordering the East Siberian Sea. Run by TPTOG.

  • Rotaurian - The nationality of Rotaur.
  • Upper Rotaur - The upper-class and rich region of Rotaur.
  • Lower Rotaur - The lower-class and poor region of Rotaur.

Veronix - An island close to the Somali basin. Does not have an official governing party (considered lawless).

  • Veroxin - The nationality of Veronix.

Aestrou - A collection of 5 man-made islands in the Atlantic Ocean. Run by TCoC.

  • Aestrouin - The general nationality of Aestrou, specific names are usually used instead (see below).
  • Dawn - One of the nations of Aestrou
    • Dawnese - The nationality of Dawn.
  • Dusk - One of the nations of Aestrou
    • Dusken - The nationality of Dusk
  • Knight - One of the nations of Aestrou
    • Knighten - The nationality of Knight
  • Tyme - One of the nations of Aestrou
    • Tymenian - The nationality of Tyme
  • Twilux - One of the nations of Aestrou
    • Twiluxan - The nationality of Twiluxan

The Outer Realms - A collection of realms outside the regular planet alignment, mostly the afterlife in its many forms.

  • Stanton - One of the afterlives, similar to 'Hell'.
    • Stantonese - The nationality of Stanton.
  • Purgatory - A temporary holding space for souls, ghosts, and those waiting for reincarnation.
  • Heather - One of the afterlives, similar to 'Heaven'.
    • Heather - The nationality of Heather
  • Cloud 9 - A realm of collective human consciousness.
  • Deadspace - A place implied to exist but not humanly understood, only known to be used by Gods (if real at all).

Tesga - Alien planet.

  • Tesgan - The nationality of Tesga.

Factions & Companies

PTO Government (TPTOG) - Long name: ‘The Protective Tyrannical Order’. One of the 2 groups that monitors Crowler. TPTOG also monitors over Rotaur. Has a poor reputation.

  • The Kristian-Brauwn Society (KBS) - A subsection of the PTO Government that specializes in technology and medicine, run by Diablo Kristian and Finch Brauwn.
  • The Species Protection Order (SPO) - A subsection of the PTO Government that specializes in protecting public services and ensuring all species live communally. Has a reputation for not treating people correctly or doing inhumane practices (like de-tusking orcs/goblins).

The Council of Crowns (TCoC) - One of the 2 groups that monitors Crowler. TCoC also monitors over Aestrou. Generally preferred by the public.
